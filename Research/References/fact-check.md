# Fact-check policy

B1 = supplied VISTRA blueprint. S1-S15 = external sources. MEASURE = project result.

Never claim without measurement:
- VISTRA FPS
- VISTRA latency
- VISTRA IoU
- collision rate
- recovery rate
- final TTC thresholds
- physical UGV performance
- benchmark winners

The VISTRA six-class ontology, risk equation, NCS thresholds, TTC thresholds, benchmark protocol and roadmap are project design specifications from B1, not universal standards.
